# Formigo Privacy Policy

_Last updated: October 24, 2026_

Formigo is a browser extension for Chrome and Firefox that fills web forms with generated test data (names, emails, phone numbers, addresses, dates, files). It is built for developers and QA engineers who test forms.

## In short

- Formigo does not collect, sell or share any personal data.
- Your settings and files stay in your browser.
- There is no analytics, tracking or advertising.

## Data collection

Formigo does not collect, transmit, sell or share any personal or sensitive user data. It does not record your browsing history or the content of the pages you visit, and it has no servers of its own.

## Local storage

Your settings, custom field rules, value templates and any sample files you upload are saved only in your browser with the extension storage API (`storage.local`). They never leave your device. You can remove them at any time by resetting the settings on the extension's settings page or by uninstalling the extension.

## Access to web pages

Formigo reads form fields on a page only to decide what kind of test data to enter, and only when you start filling from the toolbar button, a keyboard shortcut or the right-click menu. Page content is not saved or sent anywhere.

## Network requests

When the "By meaning" image mode is turned on, Formigo downloads random sample images to attach to image upload fields from:

- `randomuser.me` for profile photos;
- `picsum.photos` for other images.

These requests contain no information about you or the pages you visit. Switch "Upload images" to "Plain 15×15 PNG" in the settings to turn off all network requests.

## Permissions

Formigo asks for access to all sites because forms that need test data can be anywhere, including localhost and internal test environments. The other permissions let it fill forms in the current tab and inside frames, add right-click menu items, and keep your settings locally. None of them are used to collect data.

## Third parties

Formigo does not share data with third parties and does not use it for advertising, credit scoring or any purpose other than filling forms.
